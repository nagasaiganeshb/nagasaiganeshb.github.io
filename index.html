My story

From Machilipatnam to production AI.

The long version, chapter by chapter.

2000 – 2015

The spark — Machilipatnam

A computer my parents stretched for, HTML at school, C at thirteen.

I was born on 4 July 2000 in Machilipatnam, a coastal town in Andhra Pradesh known for its kalamkari textiles. We were a middle-class family: technology wasn't a given, but encouragement was.

When my parents saw my curiosity, they stretched their means to get me a computer. They tapped their network too — a colleague, like a brother to my father, showed me how to take part in technical competitions. Not wealthy connections; people passing on knowledge.

My first real code was HTML at Kendriya Vidyalaya, Machilipatnam. For most classmates it was just another subject; for me it was a revelation. The summer of 8th standard, another of my father's colleagues taught me C programming. I was about thirteen.

2015 – 2018

The foundation — Polytechnic, Gudlavalleru

POLYCET rank 49,458 → 2nd in the department → a state gold medal.

I entered AANM & VVRSR Polytechnic with AP POLYCET rank 49,458. By the end of year one I was section topper.

Every day my friends and I talked while waiting at the bus stop. One day: ambulances stuck at traffic signals. What if the ambulance could talk to the signal? I built the Smart Ambulance — an MCS-51 microcontroller, IR sensors, assembly language. Not for marks; the problem felt real.

Then a competition trail across three states: 1st in IoT at Bannari Amman Institute (Tamil Nadu); a loss at DKTE Ichalkaranji that taught me how to present; 2nd + special appreciation at CSESTA 2018 on one day's preparation; and at Techno-Innova, Bharati Vidyapeeth Pune, 1st in C, 1st in Java and 2nd in paper presentation — against engineering-degree students.

I finished with 96.94% — 2nd across the entire Computer Engineering department, both shifts combined. The Prathibha Award 2018 gold medal from the Government of Andhra Pradesh, presented by Chief Minister Shri N. Chandrababu Naidu garu, went to a kid whose parents had stretched for a computer years before.

2018 – 2021

University — Acharya Nagarjuna University

ECET rank 29, Team Evolvers, a flood-rescue drone and a national win.

AP ECET rank 29 in Computer Science brought me into ANU, Guntur, by lateral entry. I found the Innovation Cell and built Team Evolvers across CSE, ECE and Mechanical — weekend hackathons, LeetCode, HackerRank.

In 2019 floods hit 13 Indian states and more than 300 lives were lost. Our answer was the Smart Rescue System: computer vision on a Raspberry Pi spotting people on a live drone feed and sending their GPS coordinates over GSM to rescue teams on the ground.

At the India International Science Festival 2019 in Kolkata — 12,000+ participants, inaugurated by the Prime Minister — we were runners-up in Strategic Applications. With TCS we won TrendInno as Team Vyakta.

The goal since 2019 was the Smart India Hackathon. Problem MK93, Government of Uttarakhand. COVID delayed the finale; we coordinated remotely — and became National Winners of SIH 2020. I completed my B.Tech in September 2021 with a CGPA of 8.0, and chose startups to build real products fast.

2022 – today

MSRcosmos — building at scale

Frontend → backend lead → architect → AI / ML → leading a state government platform.

After a first stint at a product startup from July 2021, I joined MSRcosmos in January 2022 and took on a bigger role every year.

OnlineSilver — frontend developer on a greenfield e-commerce launch; the component library I built still speeds up projects. Vinci360 — as backend lead, I revived a retired system in a week and scaled it past a million records a day. MSR GenAI — my first product as architect: a GenAI SaaS where client organisations build their own agents for each department. Smart I Helper — as RAG / AI architect, an agentic AI knowledge assistant for organisations. CrowdPulse (2025) — as architect and ML engineer, the vision ideas from Smart Rescue, now reading crowd emotion across 50+ live streams in a pilot of another of the Chief Minister's visions.

Today, at MSRcosmos, I'm Technical Lead & Architect of MediaIntel, built for the Department of Information & Public Relations.

It's a full circle: in 2018, Chief Minister Shri N. Chandrababu Naidu garu presented me the Prathibha Award as a diploma student. Since then, I've built the technology behind his ideas — the CrowdPulse pilot and now MediaIntel, presented at the District Collectors' Conference as his brainchild, where I lead the technical execution.

Observation → problem → build. Every system I've shipped started with something I saw in the real world.
